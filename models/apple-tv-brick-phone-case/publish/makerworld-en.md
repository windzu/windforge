## Title

Apple TV Retro Brick Phone Case (Lightning)

## Description

Give your everyday remote a retro brick-phone silhouette. This case is designed around the 136 × 35 × 9.25 mm envelope of the second-generation Lightning Siri Remote. Other generations and the USB-C version have not been tested.

### V2 print verification and known issues

Wind has printed and assembled v2 and confirmed the front controls, charging and other functions work normally. Two assembly issues remain in these downloadable files:

- **Magnets protrude after hot-glue installation.** The pockets do not leave enough depth for the adhesive layer. The magnets sit proud and the shells cannot close flush.
- **The side button is awkward to press during installation and can fall out.** Its installation and retention need further work. The front controls function normally.

Future iterations will test deeper magnet pockets and adhesive allowance, compare joinery or snap connections that replace the magnets, and improve side-button installation and retention. These are plans; this release does not include those changes.

### Parts and compatibility

Version 2 has five printed parts: rear shell, white front frame, black front panel, antenna and side button. Print the two white parts and three black parts on separate plates; an AMS is not required. The shells align along short sliding rails and use four pairs of magnets. The separate front panel uses alignment pins and a small amount of suitable glue.

Body dimensions: 48 × 40 × 162 mm; total height including antenna: approximately 233 mm. Additional hardware: eight D4 × 2 mm disc magnets and glue compatible with your print material. Check every magnet pair's polarity before fixing it.

### Files and printing

- Five print-oriented STL files, in millimetres.
- Two generic geometry-only 3MF plates. Configure your own printer, filament and supports and slice them before printing. They are not ready-to-print Bambu print profiles.
- STEP assembly for editing, in assembly coordinates.
- Length-stop and panel pin/socket test pieces in the fit-tests folder.

Historical slicing settings: Bambu Lab P1S, 0.4 mm nozzle, PLA, 0.16 mm layers, three walls, 15% Gyroid infill and normal automatic supports. Orient the rear shell cavity upwards, front frame mating face downwards and black panel rear face downwards. Clean supports around rail undercuts and alignment pins.

Estimated total: about 3 hours 39 minutes and 94 g across two plates. These are slicing estimates; actual print date, time and material consumption were not recorded. CAD, mesh and sampled assembly-motion checks passed; the length-stop and panel pin/socket coupons were also physically tested. Numerical checks did not reveal the adhesive-depth and side-button retention issues above.

### Assembly

1. Remove supports and dry-fit the black panel into the white frame. Confirm the pins enter their sockets and the panel seats evenly.
2. Apply a small amount of suitable glue to hidden support surfaces. Keep the remote out while the glue cures; keep glue out of openings, rails and magnet pockets.
3. Test the side button's installation, movement and retention, and fit the antenna. Check magnet polarity and dry-fit the shell closure before fixing the magnets. V2 has the known side-button and hot-glue clearance issues described above.
4. Seat the remote in the rear shell, engage the front frame with a short offset and slide it into alignment. Check controls, charging access and removal.
