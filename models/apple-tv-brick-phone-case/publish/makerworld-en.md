## Title

Retro Brick Phone Case for Apple TV Siri Remote (2nd Gen, Lightning)

## Description

Give your everyday remote a retro brick-phone silhouette. This case is designed around the 136 × 35 × 9.25 mm envelope of the second-generation Lightning Siri Remote. Other generations and the USB-C version have not been tested.

Version 2 has five printed parts: rear shell, white front frame, black front panel, antenna and side button. Print the two white parts and three black parts on separate plates; an AMS is not required. The shells align along short sliding rails and use four pairs of magnets. The separate front panel uses alignment pins and a small amount of suitable glue.

Body dimensions: 48 × 40 × 162 mm; total height including antenna: approximately 233 mm. Additional hardware: eight D4 × 2 mm disc magnets and glue compatible with your print material. Check every magnet pair's polarity before fixing it.

### Files and printing

- Five print-oriented STL files, in millimetres.
- Two generic geometry-only 3MF plates. These require your own printer, filament and support settings and must be sliced before printing; they are not ready-to-print Bambu print profiles.
- STEP assembly for editing, in assembly coordinates.
- Length-stop and panel pin/socket test pieces.

Historical slicing settings: Bambu Lab P1S, 0.4 mm nozzle, PLA, 0.16 mm layers, three walls, 15% Gyroid infill and normal automatic supports. Orient the rear shell cavity upwards, front frame mating face downwards and black panel rear face downwards. Clean supports around rail undercuts and alignment pins.

Estimated total: about 3 hours 39 minutes and 94 g across two plates. These are slicing estimates; actual time and material consumption were not recorded.

### Validation

CAD, mesh and sampled assembly-motion checks passed. Wind confirmed the length-stop and panel pin/socket test pieces fit, and the white plate finished printing. Completion of the black plate, full assembly, button operation and charging remain to be documented. Renders and the remote reference geometry do not establish a successful physical assembly.

### Assembly

1. Remove supports and dry-fit the black panel into the white frame. Confirm the pins enter their sockets and the panel seats evenly.
2. Apply a small amount of glue to hidden support surfaces. Keep the remote out while the glue cures; keep glue out of openings, rails and magnet pockets.
3. Install the side button, antenna and eight magnets after checking polarity.
4. Seat the remote in the rear shell, engage the front frame with a short offset and slide it into alignment. Check controls, charging access and removal.

### Version history and credit

Version 1 used a combined two-colour front frame. Physical feedback identified excessive longitudinal movement and support material. Version 2 adds a 136.5 mm stop spacing, separates the panel and compares print orientations part by part. Versioned source files and iteration notes: https://github.com/windzu/windforge/tree/main/models/apple-tv-brick-phone-case .

The exterior was reconstructed with reference to product photographs supplied by Wind; the original product source was not recorded. The internal fit structures were implemented in this project with AI assistance. The remote shown in renders is for compatibility reference and is not included in manufacturing downloads.
